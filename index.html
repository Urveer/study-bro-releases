First-Time Setup Guide

Study Bro is an independent, non-commercial indie app. Here's how to allow it on your device in 2 clicks.

1

Run Installer

Double-click the downloaded .exe file. Windows SmartScreen may show a blue prompt.

2

Click "More info"

Click the small underline link labeled "More info" under the protection message.

3

Click "Run anyway"

A new button will appear. Click "Run anyway" to install and start studying.
